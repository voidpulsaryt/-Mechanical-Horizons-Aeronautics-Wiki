Changelog

3.2.0 · Changes since 3.1.0

The Horizon Guild update. The pack now has a story, a real economy, paid land claims, five new quest chapters and over 80 new mods, most of them for Create and for flight.

Before you update

  • Back up your world. This is a big update.
  • Land claims reset. Open Parties and Claims is replaced by FTB Chunks, and claims don't carry over. Claim your land again after updating. Claims now cost coins.
  • Map waypoints reset. JourneyMap is replaced by Xaero's Minimap and World Map.
  • Removed mods take their items with them. Anything from the 30 removed mods disappears from inventories and the world.

Highlights

  • The Horizon Logs: a story chapter in three acts about the Aerie, the city that flew above the clouds. See the story.
  • A working economy: coins, wallets, ATMs, a bank, shops and network traders. See Coins and shops.
  • Land costs money: claims are paid in coins, protections cost upkeep, land can be sold, and teams can go to war. See Land and claims.
  • More airships: Aeroworks cockpits, Create Propulsion thrusters and wings, Immersive Aircraft, Create Big Cannons and Create Radars.
  • Over 40 new Create addons, from Steam 'n' Rails to Create: New Age.
  • A new look: a custom main menu, Terralith world generation and YUNG's Better structures.
  • The official server, built in: the Multiplayer screen now opens with the Mechanical Horizons server pinned at the top, showing whether it's online, how many players are on and the ping, with a Join button. See Join the server.
  • Official hosting partner: Underline Hosting, with 20% off your first 3 months using code VOIDPULSE.

Quests

  • New story chapter, the Horizon Logs: 15 pages in three acts, plus 5 optional margin notes.
  • New chapters for Lightman's Currency, Aeroworks and Create Propulsion.
  • Five new allied-school chapters with 92 quests: PneumaticCraft, Immersive Petroleum, Big Cannons and Aircraft, Exploration (which holds the story's epilogue) and Farmer's Delight.
  • The Welcome chapter is now the Guild hub: six core disciplines power the Horizon Engine, and five optional allied schools sit around them.
  • Removed 112 filler quests and reconnected the quests that depended on them, so no progression is lost.
  • 687 quests across 17 chapters. See Quest chapters.
